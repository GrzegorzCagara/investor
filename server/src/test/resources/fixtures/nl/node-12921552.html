Trading update Q3 2026: Volume growth in the third quarter, further strengthening of our market positions 

Pieter Wolleswinkel, CEO ForFarmers: “Both total volumes and compound feed volumes showed growth in the past quarter, representing a solid performance. Operating profitability for the first nine months of the year has exceeded the already exceptionally strong performance of the previous year, a result we are very pleased with. Certainly, given that we faced higher cost levels in the third quarter as a result of higher fuel prices and low water levels in the rivers. The latter had a considerable impact on our inbound logistics, but we were able to continue supplying our farmers effectively, thanks to the great efforts of our employees and logistics partners.
In addition to organic growth, acquisitions also play an important role in strengthening our long-term market positions. In the past quarter, we expanded our activities through both the intended acquisition of STW SA, a compound feed producer in eastern Poland, and the acquisition of Advanced Ruminant Nutrition (ARN) in the United Kingdom, a specialist in feed for ruminants. In addition, the joint venture with KPS Food Group in Poland was finalised in early October.
At the Capital Markets Day on 24 November, we will provide an update on our strategy and financial objectives, as well as more insight into this joint venture.” 

Highlights Q3 2026  

  • Total volume increased by 1.4% compared with Q3 2025, on a like-for-like basis the increase was 0.7%.  
  • Compound feed volume increased by 2.3%, as a result of the consolidation of Beukelaar Diervoeders, on a like-for-like bases volumes grew 1.3%.   
  • Turnover increased by 1.6%, on a like-for-like basis turnover remained in line with Q3 2025.  
  • Gross profit increased by 3.6% to € 159.6 million. 
  • Underlying operating expenses increased by 6.8%, mainly driven by rising fuel and energy prices.  
  • Underlying EBIT consequently declined to €22.7 million (-11.7%) and underlying EBITDA declined by 5.4% to €36.9 million.  
  • ROACE1) on underlying EBIT decreased slightly from 22.0% as at 30 June 2026 to 21.5% as at 30 September 2026. 

 1) ROACE means underlying EBIT of the last 12 months divided by average capital employed over the same period

Profile ForFarmers 

ForFarmers N.V. (‘ForFarmers’) is a company offering complete feed solutions for (organic) livestock farming. With its mission statement “For the Future of Farming”, ForFarmers is committed to future-proof farming and making the agricultural sector even more sustainable. Our goal is clear: to contribute to good returns and a robust long-term earnings model. How? By leading the way with knowledge, advice, support and products on farm. Close to the farmers, solution-oriented and with an open mind to the future. The result: a contribution to affordable and sustainable food, For the Future of Farming. With annual sales of over 10 million tonnes of animal feed, ForFarmers is a leading player in Europe. The company has production operations in the Netherlands (head office), Germany, Poland and the UK and exports to various countries within and outside Europe. ForFarmers has around 3,000 employees. ForFarmers N.V. is listed on Euronext Amsterdam.
